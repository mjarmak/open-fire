Delete your OpenFIRE account

OpenFIRE is developed by Jenius Tech SRL. You can request deletion without opening the app.

Request deletion

  1. Email support@jeniusapps.com from the email address associated with your Jenius account.
  2. Use the subject Delete my OpenFIRE account and include your OpenFIRE username.
  3. State whether you want only your OpenFIRE data deleted or your centralized Jenius account deleted as well.
Request account deletion

Data that is deleted

The request removes your OpenFIRE profile link, portfolio positions, retirement and DCA settings, Telegram alert settings, and saved feedback messages. If you request deletion of your centralized Jenius account, its identity record is deleted as well.

Processing and retention

We verify requests to protect accounts from unauthorized deletion. Verified data is removed from active systems within 7 days. Encrypted backup copies expire within 30 days. We retain information longer only when required by law, and only for the legally required period.
