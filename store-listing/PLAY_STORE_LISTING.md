## Descriptions

Short description:

> Track portfolio risk, market indicators, alerts, and retirement progress.

Full description:

> OpenFIRE brings your portfolio, market-risk indicators, stock alerts, dollar-cost averaging reminders, and retirement planning into one focused dashboard.
>
> Review current positions and watchlists, inspect volatility and credit conditions, explore stock risk metrics and price history, and model retirement progress using your own savings and withdrawal assumptions.
>
> Sign in with your Jenius account to keep your OpenFIRE portfolio and settings connected across sessions. Market data availability depends on configured data providers and their current service limits.
>
> OpenFIRE is an informational planning tool. It does not execute trades and does not provide financial advice.
